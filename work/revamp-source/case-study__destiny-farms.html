A local producer with a story rooted in Western Australia’s South West. Destiny Farms brings extra virgin olive oil, infused oils and olive-oil-based products into a website that connects the place, the produce and the people behind it. The experience introduces both the product range and olive processing services for growers.

ClientDestiny Farms
Project focusFood / e-commerce

A story rooted in place

The website serves two different intentions: customers exploring olive oil and artisanal products, and growers looking for processing services. The challenge is to make both routes clear while keeping the producer’s local identity at the centre. The brand story gives context to the range, connecting the products with their origins.

From grove to product

Olive-grove imagery establishes the setting before the page introduces the farm and its range. A green accent palette and generous white space connect the agricultural character with a clear shopping presentation. Product categories separate extra virgin oil, garlic-infused oil, soaps and creams, and gift ideas, helping visitors explore the range in manageable groups.

Two clear routes

Product and contact calls to action sit together so shoppers and service enquiries each have a next step. The shop, service information and farm background remain accessible through the main navigation. Category tabs on the homepage provide another route into the catalogue while contact details keep the local business easy to reach.

The experience

Destiny Farms combines a brand introduction with practical product discovery. The place-led imagery supplies a distinctive identity, while the catalogue and service routes give visitors somewhere useful to go next. The presentation leaves room for the farm’s story without losing sight of the products and services people came to find.
