Fashion / e-commerce

Peter Webber

An established South Australian retailer, brought into an online shopping experience. Peter Webber brings menswear, big men’s clothing and womenswear together with a focus on everyday style, familiar brands and personal service. The website connects that retail identity with collection browsing, product discovery and clear routes into the store’s range.

Client
Peter Webber
Project focus
Fashion / e-commerce

The shopping journey

For a store with several audiences, the first task is helping each shopper find the right starting point. Menswear, big men’s clothing and womenswear need distinct entry points while remaining part of one recognisable brand. Seasonal ranges, sale collections and brand-led browsing create different ways into the catalogue without asking every visitor to shop in the same way.

A collection-led experience

Large category imagery and direct shopping links introduce the range before the page moves into featured collections. The presentation puts clothing and photography first, with supporting copy providing context rather than competing with the merchandise. A consistent visual hierarchy connects the main departments, promotional areas and the wider product catalogue.

The practical details

Search, account access and a visible cart support the shopping journey beyond the homepage. Collection links give returning customers a route to new arrivals and familiar departments. Delivery information, returns, contact details and store background sit alongside the retail content, helping visitors find practical answers while they explore.

The experience

The result is a retail presentation built around choice: browse by department, explore a collection or look for a specific item. The brand’s local identity and established store presence provide context for the online catalogue, bringing the relationship between physical retail and digital shopping into the same experience.
