Pawzody

Jennifer Wells, founder of Pawzody, wanted a modern and trustworthy website that would reflect her brand professionally and make it easier for customers to discover services, view previous work, and submit inquiries. The goal was to create a clean online presence that felt welcoming, easy to navigate, and optimized for both desktop and mobile users.

Client
Jennifer Wells / Pawzody
Services
Brand, Web, Marketing
Project
2025

The challenge

The previous website lacked a clear structure and did not effectively communicate the quality of the brand. Important information was difficult to find, service pages were not organized properly, and the overall visual presentation felt outdated. In addition, the site needed to perform better across mobile devices and provide a smoother user experience for potential customers.

What we did

We redesigned the website with a fresh and elegant layout focused on usability, visual appeal, and conversion. The new design included:

  • Clear service sections so visitors could quickly understand what Pawzody offers.
  • Improved navigation to help users move through the site effortlessly.
  • Professional imagery and branding that created a more polished and trustworthy appearance.
  • Strong call-to-action sections encouraging visitors to request information and get in touch.
  • Mobile-friendly layouts to ensure a consistent experience across all devices.

Implementation

The website was built in WordPress using a responsive design approach. Every section was optimized for desktops, tablets, and smartphones. Performance improvements included cleaner page structures, optimized images, and SEO-friendly content formatting to improve visibility and loading speed.

The outcome

The final website provided Jennifer Wells with a polished digital presence that accurately represents the Pawzody brand. Visitors can now easily browse services, explore previous projects, and contact the business with confidence. The improved design, navigation, and mobile responsiveness create a better overall user experience and help support future business growth.
